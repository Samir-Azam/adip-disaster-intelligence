# Project Charter

## Vision
ADIP turns fragmented disaster reports, geospatial information, imagery, forecasts, and resource data into prioritized, explainable operational information.

## Initial scenario
Flood response is the primary scenario. The architecture should support future wildfire and landslide modules.

## Users
- Citizen
- Volunteer
- Field officer
- District administrator
- State administrator
- NGO/relief coordinator

## MVP
1. Authentication and RBAC
2. Incident reporting and verification
3. GIS operations map
4. Evidence/image upload
5. Asynchronous AI image assessment
6. Explainable incident priority recommendation
7. Resource inventory and assignment
8. Shelters and hospitals
9. Real-time incident updates
10. Notifications
11. Audit log
12. Admin analytics
13. Dockerized local environment
14. Tests and CI

## Out of scope
Autonomous dispatch, medical diagnosis, guaranteed prediction, production government integration, and claims of official certification.
